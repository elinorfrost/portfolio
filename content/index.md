## Bio

I am a Master of Health Informatics student at the University of Michigan School of Information and School of Public Health. I am currently working as a research assistant for the Health Justice Informatics Lab under Dr. Megan Threats. I am interested in the intersection of medical sociology and information science, with particular interest in the experiences of Black and Indigenous women in the US Healthcare system. 

I received my Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science and Sociology from Oberlin College in 2025. Whilst pursuing my degree, I worked as a research assistant for the Oberlin Computer Science Department under professor Adam Eck, where I collected, managed, and analyzed data from prompt-engineering tasks focusing on survey data collection quality. Outside of the lab, I was a collegiate swimmer and pole vaulter, and I now enjoy swimming (recreationally!) and spending time with my dog, Melon.
