The Health Justice Informatics Lab is directed by Dr. Megan Threats, PhD, MSLIS. Dr. Threats is an Assistant Professor of Information at the University of Michigan School of Information. 

> [!quote] The Mission
> 
> The Health Justice Informatics Lab at the University of Michigan is dedicated to using community-based methods to detect, understand, and reduce health information and technology inequities among marginalized communities. We strive to build equitable partnerships with community members and institutional partners to co-create information resources and programs that improve the health and well-being of our communities. 
> 
> View our research and resources at www.healthjusticelab.com

## My role in the lab
**October 2025 - Present**

As a research assistant, I perform various qualitative and quantitative tasks to further the lab's mission of bringing information resources to our community to foster health and well-being. Some of my responsibilities include: 

- Interview transcription and coding
- Community resource content creation 
- survey data visualization and analysis
