## Overview
**October 2025 - Present**

*Breast Cancer Support* is an independent project. I want to learning basic web scraping to collect data for potential machine learning or statistical modeling projects. I am using python's `requests` and `bs4` to scrape user posts and comments from the _SHARE Metastatic Breast Cancer_ page within _Health Unlocked_, a support forum for people to share their experiences and find community with individuals who experience similar disease and illnesses.

*This is a work in progress!* I hope to have a more thorough project description soon, but the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/elinorfrost/BreastCancerSupport) will have the most updated code!
