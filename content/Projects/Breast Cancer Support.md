## Overview

This is an independent project for learning basic web scraping to collect data for potential machine learning or statistical modeling projects. I plan to scrape user posts and comments from the _SHARE Metastatic Breast Cancer_ page within _Health Unlocked_, a support forum for people to share their experiences and find community with individuals who experience similar disease and illnesses.
