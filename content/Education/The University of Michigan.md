### Master of Health Informatics
**Fall 2025 - Spring 2027 (Anticipated)**

I found a degree program to combine my interests of medical sociology with data/information science. Though I attended school in Ohio, Michigan has always been my home. 

### Courses
**Fall 2025**
- SQL and Databases
	- This more formally introduced me to querying data from relational database models, and principles of designing workable databases
- Introduction to Health Informatics
	- A survey of the domains of health informatics, including clinical, biomedical, consumer health, public health, and foundations of health information exchange
- Topics in Health Informatics (Program Seminar)
- Problem-Solving with People, Information, and Technology
- Statistics and Data Analysis
	- Haven taken statistics before, I utilized this class as an opportunity to build upon my understanding of data modeling and visualization with R
- Introduction to Programming
	- This class went deeper into web interaction, information representation, and APIs than I had with my undergraduate major in Computer Science
