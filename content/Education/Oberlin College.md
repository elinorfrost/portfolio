I graduated from Oberlin College in May of 2025 with my Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science and Sociology, with a concentration in Data Science.  

### Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science
**Fall 2021 - Spring 2025**

I declared my first major in Computer Science shortly after taking Introduction to Programming in my first semester at Oberlin. Python was my first programming language, and it remains my favorite. 
##### Courses
- Introduction to Programming - Python
- Data Structures - Java
- Discrete Mathematics 
- Systems Programming - C
- Programming Abstractions - Racket
	- I was a Tutor and Grader for 2 years after taking this class
- Computer Architecture
- Algorithms
- Theory of Computation
- Human-Centered Security
- Applied Machine Learning - Python

### Bachelor of Arts in Sociology
**Fall 2021 - Spring 2025**

I decided to declare my second major in Sociology after I had taken three courses and loved the content I learned in all of them. Medical Sociology was my introduction to the US healthcare system, and how I became interested in the sociology of health.

I finished my degree with a research project investigating my peers' experiences with sexual health education, and how they navigate personal relationships. 
##### Courses
- Introduction to Sociology: An Analysis of Society
- Autoethnography and the Life Course
- Introduction to Statistics
- Medical Sociology
- Sociology of Sexualities
- Critical Criminology
- Classical and Contemporary Sociological Theory
- Social Research Methods
##### Awards
- **Comfort Starr Award**, 2025 - *For academic achievement and contributions to the department*
